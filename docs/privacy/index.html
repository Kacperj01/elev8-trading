Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy explains what ELEV8 Trading ("ELEV8", "we") collects on this website, why, and your rights. The ELEV8 Journal app at elev8tradingjournal.com has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no cookies, no advertising trackers and no analytics. The only personal data we collect is what you send us in the mentorship application form:

  • your name and email address
  • your Instagram or Discord username, if you add one
  • your trading level and what you want to get out of mentorship
  • that you confirmed you're 18 or over and agreed to this policy, and the time you applied

Why we use it

We use your application only to review it and contact you about mentorship. Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into an agreement with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: stores mentorship applications in our database.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts this website. Like any web host, it handles technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they use safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Other sites we link to

Discord, Telegram, Instagram, Vantage and the ELEV8 Journal are separate services with their own privacy policies. If you open a Vantage account through our partner link, Vantage may tell us that an account was opened through it so that we can pay or receive commission and give you Telegram access.

How long we keep it

We keep mentorship applications for up to 12 months, then delete them. If you become a mentorship client, we keep what we need to provide the mentorship and meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or ask for a copy of it. Email privacy@elev8tradingjournal.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland at dataprotection.ie, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Age

ELEV8's mentorship, signals and community are only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
