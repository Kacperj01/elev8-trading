FAQ

Good
questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Message us on Instagram or in the Discord.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutely. The Beginner level of the mentorship and the free Discord are made for people starting from zero.

How does mentorship work?

Fill in the application form. Kacper reads every application and replies by email to talk about your level, your goals, the format and the price.

How much does it cost?

The free Discord, the Telegram signals (through our Vantage link) and the journal are free. Mentorship and premium Discord prices are being finalised. Apply or join the free Discord to hear first.

How do I get the Telegram signals?

Open a Vantage account using the ELEV8 partner link, send us your account ID on Instagram or in the Discord, and we'll add you to the channel.

Is the trading journal really free?

Yes. The ELEV8 Journal lives at elev8tradingjournal.com and is free for everyone.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything ELEV8 shares, including mentorship, signals and community posts, is for education only. You make your own decisions and you're responsible for your own risk.
